The journey

From a school kid experimenting on a timeline to a storyteller behind the lens. Scroll the reel.

1
Chapter 1, school days

Where the cutting began

It started at school. I trimmed clips, synced them to music and experimented with transitions on whatever device I had. I didn't know it yet, but I was learning rhythm, the heartbeat of every great edit.

Learned timing, music sync, transitions

2
Chapter 2, college

Falling for the frame

In college I picked up a camera and everything changed. Photography and videography pulled me in: light, composition, and the feeling of capturing a moment exactly as it felt.

Learned photography, videography, composition

3
Chapter 3, the work

Polishing every skill

Shoot after shoot and project after project, I sharpened my eye behind the lens and my hands on the timeline, learning camera movement, lighting and story structure the real way: by doing it.

Learned cinematography, lighting, storytelling

4
Chapter 4, today

Editing, perfected

The cutting that started at school is now a refined, signature editing style. Add professional colour grading and AI video, and every project gets a complete, cinematic finish.

Now editing, colour grading, AI video
